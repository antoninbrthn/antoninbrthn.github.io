I am a PhD student in Machine Learning at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Prof. Mihaela van der Schaar. My research focuses on post-training methods for LLMs, particularly on how pretrained models can continually acquire new, generalisable capabilities and skills. I am also interested in using LLMs to build AI systems that learn, reason, and adapt in ways closer to human learning than conventional machine learning methods, with a particular interest in AI for education.

Before starting my PhD, I spent over four years developing machine learning methods for neural and physiological time-series data at a neurotechnology company (BIOS Health). I was also a research intern at RIKEN AIP, working on weakly supervised learning and learning with noisy labels.
